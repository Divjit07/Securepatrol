## 🔴 The iPhone NFC problem — decide before you pitch

Your own app says it plainly: **checkpoint NFC taps require an Android phone.** iOS
doesn't expose Web NFC, so until the Capacitor native wrap ships, an iPhone guard can
clock in (GPS) but **cannot tap checkpoints.**

This is a live sales risk, not a slide problem. Before pitching, decide:

- **If the client's guards are yours and you issue the phones** — buy cheap Android
  handsets. Problem disappears, and it never comes up.
- **If guards use their own phones (BYOD — which your app labels them as)** — some will
  have iPhones, and patrol verification silently won't work for them. Either mandate
  Android for patrol staff, or ship the native wrap first.
- **Do not** let a client discover this after signing. Getting ahead of it — "we issue
  Android devices for patrol" — turns a weakness into a competence signal.
